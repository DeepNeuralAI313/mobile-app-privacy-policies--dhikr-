Privacy Policy for Quran & Tasbih Dhikr Tracker

Last updated November 27, 2026

DeepNeuralAI Technologies provides Quran & Tasbih Dhikr Tracker (Android package com.dhikrapp.qurantracker). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data the app uses

The app stores your Quran bookmarks and reading progress, Dhikr counts and session history, custom Dhikr, guided Adhkar progress, daily goals, reminders and preferences on your device. It uses this information to display your progress, run your reminders and update the optional home-screen widget. The app uses your device's time zone to schedule reminders.

We do not receive your in-app activity or require an account. The app has no advertising, analytics or developer-operated cloud sync.

Backups, exports and sharing

You can save or restore a JSON backup, export progress as a CSV file, or share a backup using the device's share menu. These files can contain your tracking history and other information you entered. The location, app or recipient you choose may receive and retain the file under its own privacy practices. The developer does not receive a copy through these features.

Your operating system may also back up or transfer app data depending on your device and backup settings.

Notifications and updates

If you enable reminders and allow notifications, the app schedules them on your device. On Android, the app can reschedule reminders after a restart or app update. The app uses Google Play to check for Android updates. Google handles Play Store update requests under its own Google Privacy Policy. The app does not send your Dhikr or Quran tracking records to Google Play for update checks.

Security, retention and deletion

The app uses the operating system's app-private storage for its database and preferences. Exported files can be read by anyone with access to the location where you save them. The app keeps its local records until you remove them or clear its storage. Settings allows you to reset progress. You can clear all local app data in your device settings or by uninstalling the app. Manage exported files and device backups separately through the app, storage location or backup service where you saved them.

Privacy contact

Email DeepNeuralAI Technologies at deepneuralaidna@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests. If you email us, we use the information you provide to respond and retain it as needed to handle your request.
